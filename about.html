Reviving Classical Astronomical Horology in the Modern Era

CosmicGearLine was founded upon an uncompromising vision: that a mechanical wristwatch should serve as a functional, miniature planetarium tracking our living connection to celestial mechanics.

Atelier Genesis

From Tower Clocks to Wrist-Mounted Astronomical Orreries

Throughout Renaissance Europe, master clockmakers like Giovanni de Dondi and Jost Bürgi engineered monumental astraria that mechanically represented planetary motions, zodiacal constellations, and church feast calculations through intricate gear trains.

In the centuries that followed, the industrialization of horology gradually simplified timekeeping into uniform twelve-hour civil increments. CosmicGearLine brings this forgotten majesty back into the modern era, condensing astronomical differential gearing, sidereal escapements, and real-time planispheres into wearable wrist sculptures engineered for generations.

Core Pillars

1. Complete In-House Engineering

From the initial kinematic gearing simulations to movement blank cutting and heat-treatment, every mechanical step is executed within our own workshops.

4. Generational Permanence

Constructed from non-degrading noble metals, titanium, and meteorite, every CosmicGearLine watch is fully mechanical and built to be serviced indefinitely.
