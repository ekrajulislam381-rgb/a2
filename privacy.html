Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Version: 3.4 Governance Jurisdiction: State of New York, United States

1. Information We Collect

We collect strictly necessary personal data provided during bespoke horological inquiries, salon appointment scheduling, and private dispatch registration. This includes your name, private email address, phone contact, delivery domicile, and movement customization preferences.

Client data is used solely to execute horological commissions, conduct chronometric warranty validations, issue private invitations to limited-edition unveilings, and ensure secure, insured transit of commissioned timepieces. We never sell, monetize, or disclose client identity to third parties.

All digital transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted utilizing industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Stored records are maintained in isolated, access-restricted database systems with continuous vulnerability scanning.

Under international data protection frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation and California Consumer Privacy Act, clients maintain the right to inspect, correct, export, or permanently erase their personal records from our databases upon formal request to privacy@cosmicgearline.com.
